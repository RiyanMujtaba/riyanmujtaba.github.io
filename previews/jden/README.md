# J'DEN SMASH

Single-page site for **J'DEN SMASH** — smash burgers, smashed to order. Karachi & Hyderabad.

Built around the real brand: sleek black & white, photography-led, minimal — matching the
white script logo and the club's clean social aesthetic.

## Real details
- **Tagline:** Smash Burgers · never frozen · smashed to order
- **Signature:** 520 Smash · also Oklahoma Smash & Signature Sides
- **Hours:** Open 6PM until sold out
- **Locations:** Padel SmashX Bahadurabad (Karachi) · Gohar Green Society Malir (Karachi) · Autobahn Padel Core (Hyderabad)
- **Instagram:** [@jden.pk](https://www.instagram.com/jden.pk/)
